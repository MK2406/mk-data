1

What MK does not collect

MK has no server and no accounts. The app does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. There are no ads and no analytics. The only thing that ever reaches MK's maker is feedback you choose to send (section 7).

2

eCampus login

When you connect eCampus, you type your roll number and password on the official eCampus web page shown inside the app. MK never reads, sees or stores your password. eCampus keeps you logged in with its own cookie, stored only on your phone.

3

Data stored on your phone

After you log in, MK reads your attendance and timetable from eCampus and saves them on your phone. Your own edits are also saved only on your phone:

  • subject names, short forms, rooms and staff
  • notes and your own plans
  • your name and settings
This data is excluded from cloud backups and device transfers.
4

Data MK downloads

MK downloads one public file with the mess menu, academic calendar, exam dates, announcements, help answers and the latest app version number. Downloading it sends no personal information.

The file is hosted on GitHub. Like any website, GitHub can see the internet (IP) address of the phone that downloads it.

5

Reminders and announcements

Class, exam and mess reminders are set on your phone by MK itself. To check for new announcements, MK downloads the same public file about every 30 minutes. Nothing about you is sent.

Sound and vibration are played by MK on your phone. You can turn them off, and MK stays quiet on silent, in Do Not Disturb and, if you choose, at night. You can turn each kind of notification on or off in Account.

6

Links and updates

Links in announcements, the feedback form and MK's social pages open in your browser or in that app. Those websites have their own privacy policies. MK only opens secure (https) links and shows you the website name first.

When a new version of MK is out, MK shows an “Update available” message by comparing its own version number with the public file. Updates come from the Google Play Store.

7

Feedback

Send feedback opens a Google Form in your browser. To help fix problems, the form is pre-filled with your MK version, Android version and phone model. You can see and change this before sending, and nothing is sent unless you submit the form.

Feedback is stored in MK's Google Forms account and used only to fix and improve MK. Google's own privacy policy applies to Google Forms.

8

Deleting your data

  • Account → Disconnect logs you out of eCampus and deletes your attendance and timetable.
  • Account → Clear everything deletes all of MK's data on your phone.
  • Uninstalling MK deletes everything it stored.
