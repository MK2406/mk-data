MK – Campus Companion: Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

MK is an unofficial app made by a student for students. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or run by PSG Institutions.

What MK does not collect

MK has no server and no accounts. It does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. There are no ads and no analytics.

eCampus login

When you connect eCampus, you type your roll number and password on the official eCampus web page shown inside the app. MK never reads, sees or stores your password. eCampus keeps you logged in with its own cookie, stored only on your phone.

Data stored on your phone

After you log in, MK reads your attendance and timetable from eCampus and saves them on your phone. Your own edits (room and staff, notes, plans, settings) are also saved only on your phone. This data is excluded from cloud backups and device transfers.

Data MK downloads

MK downloads one public file with the mess menu, academic calendar and announcements. Downloading it sends no personal information.

Deleting your data

Class, exam and mess reminders are set on your phone by MK itself. To check for new announcements, MK downloads the same public file about once an hour; nothing about you is sent.

In MK, go to Account → Disconnect to log out of eCampus and delete its data. Uninstalling MK deletes everything it stored.
