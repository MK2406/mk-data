What MK does not collect

MK has no server and no accounts. It does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. There are no ads and no analytics.

eCampus login

When you connect eCampus, you type your roll number and password on the official eCampus web page shown inside the app. MK never reads, sees or stores your password. eCampus keeps you logged in with its own cookie, stored only on your phone.

Data stored on your phone

After you log in, MK reads your attendance and timetable from eCampus and saves them on your phone. Your own edits are also saved only on your phone:

  • subject names, short forms, rooms and staff
  • notes and your own plans
  • your name and settings

This data is excluded from cloud backups and device transfers.

Data MK downloads

MK downloads one public file with the mess menu, academic calendar, exam dates and announcements. Downloading it sends no personal information.

Reminders and announcements

Class, exam and mess reminders are set on your phone by MK itself. To check for new announcements, MK downloads the same public file about once an hour. Nothing about you is sent. You can turn each kind of notification on or off in Account.

Deleting your data

  • Account → Disconnect logs you out of eCampus and deletes your attendance and timetable.
  • Account → Clear everything deletes all of MK's data on your phone.
  • Uninstalling MK deletes everything it stored.
